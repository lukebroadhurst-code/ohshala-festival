Our story

In 2019 we had a vision: the first wellbeing festival in Buckinghamshire.

Oh Shala came together through a group of people who wanted more of a sense of community around Marlow. We soon realised the surrounding corners of Buckinghamshire and Berkshire were full of hidden treasures of wellbeing, and we wanted to share them far and wide.

We think of it as a gathering more than a festival: grassroots, local, and about connection. A safe, non-judgmental space where you can express yourself however you need to.

Each year we highlight local charities: Marlow Refugee Action, One Can Trust (Buckinghamshire’s food bank), War Child UK and Dash Charity.

We welcome you with open arms to embrace community and celebrate nature.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Can’t see yours? Read the full FAQs or write to hello@ohshalafestival.com.

What do I need to bring?

Your yoga mat, warm clothing for the evenings, and your own reusable bottle, mug and cutlery. You’re welcome to bring your own food and drink too.

Can we camp on site?

Yes, your festival ticket includes free camping. Glamping and vehicle camping are booked separately.

Can I bring my children?

Absolutely. Children go free, with limited availability, and need adult supervision at all times. Check the tickets page for current age bands.

Can I bring alcohol?

No. We have a strict no alcohol or drugs policy. Prohibited substances are confiscated and may mean removal from site.

Can I leave and come back?

Yes, as long as you have your valid wristband.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets are non-refundable, but you can transfer yours to someone else by contacting us.

What facilities are there?

Toilets, showers and free water taps. Vendors sell food and treatments separately.

Where can I get help on the day?

Our staff are on site to answer questions, and there’s a staffed support van.
